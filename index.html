About the At Shades franchise

What it means to be an At Shades franchisee.

An At Shades franchise is a locally owned business that sells, measures and installs custom interior and exterior window coverings under the At Shades brand. Each franchisee is granted a defined territory and the right to operate using the At Shades name, marks and methods.

At Shades provides franchisees with initial and ongoing training, an operations manual, quoting and measuring software, product fabrication, marketing and lead generation, and field support. Franchisees provide the local presence: in-home consultations, measuring, sales and installation for residential, commercial and hospitality customers.

We are looking for owner-operators who want to build a trade business in their community. No prior window-covering experience is required — every new At Shades franchisee completes our training program before opening.
